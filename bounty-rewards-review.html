How rewards work

Three layers

1 · Weekly points

Finish the bounty, get points. The first finisher gets the big number, everyone after gets the small one. 1 point = 500k GP in the shop.

EASY20 / 15
MEDIUM30 / 20
HARD45 / 25
ELITE70 / 35

Example: finishing an Easy bounty after someone else still pays 15 pts (7.5M). If nobody claims within 3 days, the first claim gets ×1.5.

2 · Grand Bounty

150M → 300M

Admins pick which bounties are Grand Bounties. The first player to finish one wins 150M GP on top of their points. If a Grand Bounty week goes unclaimed, the next one is worth +50M, up to 300M. The pot shows on the board.

Today: Telos and Zamorak. That is about 5 Grand Bounty weeks and 1 winner a year, so the prize is usually 250–300M. More will be ticked over time.

3 · Monthly draw

100M

Every claim that month is 1 ticket, whatever the tier. An Easy claim counts as much as an Elite one, so mid-level players have a real chance.

12 winners a year. First draw 1 Jan, with December claims.

What it costs

Full package: about 300M a month, at most

10 active players, 4 hours each a week on the bounty. "At most" because it assumes every point gets spent in the shop. Unspent points and wheel losses make the real cost lower.

Mid-level player

Can do Low and Mid bosses only. The flatter scale almost doubles what they earn. With 6 more Easy/Medium bounties it rises to about 7.4.

Endgame player

11.5 → 12.2 pts/week

Elite first claims pay less (100 → 70), but endgame players still earn a little more overall, and the Grand Bounties (150M+) are aimed at them.

Simulated with 5 mid-level and 5 endgame players, 4 hours each a week. Mid-level players still have nothing to do in about 58% of weeks, because 11 of the 20 bounties are endgame bosses. More Easy and Medium bounties is the fix for that.

Every bounty and its tier

Tier = average hours to finish (wiki drop rate ÷ kills per hour): up to 2 h Easy, 2–6 Medium, 6–15 Hard, over 15 Elite. A High or Very high difficulty boss moves it one tier up. Today: 2 Easy, 3 Medium, 6 Hard, 9 Elite.

How the numbers were worked out
  • Each week one of the 20 bounties is picked at random. A 20,000-week simulation estimates who finishes and when.
  • A player who spends 4 hours finishes with chance 1 − e−4 / hours to finish, times the share of players who can do the boss at all (Low 100%, Mid 80%, High 50%, Very high 25%).
  • Drop chances are per kill, counting every drop pile, from the RS3 wiki (read 21 Nov 2026). Kills per hour from the wiki money-making guides, or our own numbers in the Difficulty tab.
  • Grand Bounty: an unclaimed Grand Bounty week adds 50M to the next one, up to 300M. The pot resets when someone wins. Cost simulated with Telos and Zamorak ticked.
  • Not counted: Pick next week's bounty, sponsors, duels, gifts, donations. They move points between players and cost the clan nothing.
  • Old bounties: 150M paid every week ≈ 650M a month.

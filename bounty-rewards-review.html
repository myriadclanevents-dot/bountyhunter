Bounty rewards review

Our old bounties paid 100M, 150M, sometimes 200M to the winner. The new points system spreads the rewards thinner. It is fairer, but nobody ever feels "omg, I want to win this". This page shows what the current setup really pays out, then gives five options with their costs, so we can pick a budget and a feel together.

All numbers assume 10 active players (change it in the bar below). Points are valued at 500k GP each, the rate our shop uses. Drop chances and kills per hour come from the RS3 wiki (read 21 Nov 2026), counted per kill, including every drop pile a kill gives. The difficulty table shows which game mode and enrage each number is for.

The options

Five ways to set it up

A and B only change numbers in the sheet. C and D add a big GP prize on top of the points. Our recommendation is B + C + D together.

How to read "5/2": the first number is the points for the first player to finish the bounty. The second is the points for each player who finishes after that. Everyone can claim once. These are the sheet's First Points and Later Points columns. Example: on an Easy bounty the first finisher gets 5 points and every later finisher gets 2.

Difficulty

Pay for difficulty, not for the boss

Until 21 Nov almost every bounty paid 10 / 5, whatever it asked. Each bounty now has a tier built from two things, and its points come from that tier. The tiers live in the sheet's Difficulty tab: admins can change a number or a boss rating there and run applyDifficultyToBounties to update the Bounties tab.

1 · Drop luck: hours to finish

The chance per kill of getting what the bounty asks for, counting every drop pile in a kill: Kerapac gives 3 piles, so 3 × 1/760 becomes 1/253 per kill. "Any of 4 items" adds up the 4 chances. When 2 or 3 parts are all required, we count the average kills until you have all of them. Average kills ÷ kills per hour from the wiki's money making guides = average hours to finish.

2 · Boss difficulty

Low: safe or mid-level bosses (QBD, Ivar, Gate, Croesus). Mid: needs good gear and a few mechanics. High: endgame, mechanics-heavy, or needs a team. Very high: top-end enrage bosses (Telos, Zamorak, Ghrazi). The rating comes from the wiki's requirements and fight notes. We also use it to estimate how many of our active players can do the boss at all: Low 100%, Mid 80%, High 50%, Very high 25%.

For us to review

Points we propose to review

1 · Three bounties were easier than they looked

Vorago included Tectonic energy, which drops almost every kill. Arch-Glacor asked for "Manuscript of Wen", a common extra drop. Queen Black Dragon: all 4 royal crossbow pieces take about 26 kills, roughly an hour.

Done: Tectonic energy is removed and Arch-Glacor now asks for Scripture of Wen. QBD stays as it is, as an Easy bounty.

2 · Some are close to impossible in one week

Legiones: Ascension grips are 1 in 2,056 Legio kills and only drop on a Slayer task. Telos at 100% enrage: about 1 in 1,100 for any dormant weapon. Ghrazi in normal mode: codex 1 in 3,000. Profane Scabarite: lens 1 in 4,096 off task (1 in 512 on task).

Replace Legiones grips (e.g. "any Ascension signet", about 1 in 49). For the others, state the enrage or Slayer task in the bounty and pay Elite.

3 · Write the mode, enrage and team size into the bounty

The same item has very different odds. Zuk and Vorago drop twice as often in hard mode. Zamorak's codex goes from 1 in 606 (under 20% enrage) to 1 in 78 (2,000%). The Ambassador crossbow roll is 1 in 55 solo but 1 in 166 in a trio. Croesus depends on your contribution.

Add a line to Notes, e.g. "any mode" or "hard mode only", "solo or group". Our tiers assume the mode named in the table.

4 · Done: pay now follows the work

Before, QBD (about 1 hour) and Zamorak's Chaos Roar codex (about 28 hours at 100% enrage) both paid 10/5. Now QBD pays 8/3 and Zamorak 100/40. Nine of our 20 bounties are Elite.

Still open: the Settings Multiplier is ×2. Option B assumes ×1.5. The Elite bounties are the natural home for the Grand Bounty prize.

5 · The ×2 after 72 hours mostly hits hard bounties

On Hard and Elite bounties nobody claims in the first 3 days quite often, so the ×2 becomes a hidden price doubling.

Lower it to ×1.5 (Settings → Multiplier = 1,5), or keep ×2 and price it in.

6 · Can players afford the shop?

The shop table shows how many weeks of play each item costs under each option. If the 100M item takes most of a year, it is a goal for very few players.

Raise the points (option B), or accept that the shop is for small items and the big moments come from C and D.

7 · Wheel prizes and descriptions

The 4% prize "50M" still has the description "Your own colour and name". Points prizes (+5, +20) only hand points back, which makes the odds harder to read.

Fix the description. Consider the GP-only wheel shown above.

8 · "Dye of choice" items

Two 115-point items say "Dye of choice". A Third Age or Blood dye is worth billions, not 57M.

Rename them to "ordinary dye" or list the allowed dyes.

9 · Unspent points are money we owe

Every point in a wallet is a promise of 500k. If many players save up, a large bill can arrive at once.

Keep Stock limits on the GP items (the 100M already has 3) and check the total of all wallets once a month.

10 · Agree the budget first

The old bounties cost about – a month (set the old prize and frequency above). Everything here scales with one number: what the sponsor and the clan bank can put in each month.

Agree a monthly ceiling, then pick the option that fits under it.

How these numbers were worked out
  • Each week one bounty is picked at random from the 20 rows in the sheet.
  • For each bounty: average hours to finish = average kills needed ÷ kills per hour (see the difficulty table). A player who spends H hours on it finishes with chance 1 − e−H / hours, multiplied by the share of players who can do that boss (Low 100%, Mid 80%, High 50%, Very high 25%).
  • Drop chances are per kill and include every drop pile (Kerapac 3, Arch-Glacor normal mode 3, Vorago 5 piles shared by the team). When several parts are all needed, the average kills is the expected wait until you hold one of each.
  • Finish times are spread over the week, so the first claim gets the multiplier when nobody finishes in the first 72 hours. Results come from a 20,000-week simulation in this page.
  • "Before" uses the First/Later Points the sheet had until 21 Nov, with ×2 after 72h. Option B is what the sheet has now. Kills per hour and boss ratings come from the Difficulty tab (read 21 Nov 2026).
  • Grand Bounty: an Elite week nobody claims adds 50M to the next Elite prize, up to 300M. Monthly draw: a claim gives 1 / 2 / 4 / 8 tickets (Easy → Elite), and the first claim gets double. The cost is simply the prize. "Chance to win a big prize" assumes equal players.
  • Not counted: Pick next week's bounty, sponsors, duels, gifts and donations. They move points between players and cost the clan nothing.
  • Sources: the boss pages and "Money making guide" pages on runescape.wiki, read 21 Nov 2026.
